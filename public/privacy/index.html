Privacy Policy

mcp.vin decodes vehicle identification numbers using public data. You can use the website, the REST API and the MCP server without an account. We do not show ads, we do not use analytics or tracking cookies, and we do not sell or rent data to anyone.

mcp.vin is operated by Matthew Broudy. This policy covers the website at mcp.vin, the REST API under mcp.vin/api, the hosted MCP server at mcp.vin/mcp, and the mcp.vin app. Questions and requests go to privacy@mcp.vin.

What we collect

When you look up a vehicle

We receive the VIN or the make, model and year you send. A VIN identifies a vehicle, not a person, and we do not try to link it to an owner. To answer a lookup we keep the result in server memory for up to one hour, so repeated lookups are fast.

Request logs

For every request we record the IP address, the time, the method and path (the path of a lookup contains the VIN), the response status and size, how long it took, the browser or client name, and your account number if you are signed in. We use these logs to keep the service running, to apply rate limits and to stop abuse.

When you report a decode error

The report and the car sheet check the official decode against other public records and mark values that are corrected, doubtful or filled in. If a decoded value is wrong for your vehicle, “Report a decode error” lets you say which field and what your vehicle has. We store the field, the value, how you know it (if you choose one) and the note you enter, what the VIN decodes to (model year, make, model, trim), what the report showed for that field (the official value, the checked value and its status), the first 11 characters of the VIN only (not its serial number), and the time. The report itself stores no IP address and no account. Like every request, the request log below records that one was sent. Reports are read by us before any use, are never shown to other readers and never change a value on the site by themselves. Please leave personal details out of the note.

When you send a placard or fuel tank correction

The car sheet can show tire, pressure and fuel tank values from a vehicle NHTSA tested (crash or compliance test). If yours differ, “Doesn’t match your door placard?” lets you send your own values. We store the tire sizes, cold pressures, fuel tank size and note you enter, what the VIN decodes to (model year, make, model, trim, body, drive and powertrain), which NHTSA test vehicles the sheet showed and how closely they matched, the first 11 characters of the VIN only (not its serial number), and the time. The correction itself stores no IP address and no account. Like every request, the request log below records that one was sent. Corrections are read by us before any use, are never shown to other readers and never change a value on the site by themselves. Please leave personal details out of the note.

When you create an account

An account is optional. If you create one we store your email address, a display name if you give one, and your password in a salted, hashed form that cannot be read back. We also store the vehicles you save, the labels you give them and your report settings. We do not send marketing email.

When you tell us something about your vehicle

Some details are not in the VIN. When EPA lists a model with both an automatic and a manual transmission and the VIN does not say which, the report assumes an automatic and lets you say “Mine is a manual” (or confirm the automatic). If you are signed in and the vehicle is saved, that choice is stored with the saved vehicle in your account and removed with it. If you are signed in and the vehicle is not saved, the choice is kept in your browser for that visit only, until you save the vehicle with it. If you are not signed in, the choice stays in your browser’s local storage, next to your recent reports, and is never sent to us. The REST API and MCP server take the transmission as a request option; it changes that one answer and is not stored (the request log keeps the path, not the query).

In your browser

The website keeps three things in your browser’s local storage: a sign-in token, which expires after 24 hours, the list of your recent reports, and, if you are not signed in, the transmission you chose for a vehicle (see above). The recent list and those choices never leave your browser; clearing your recent reports clears the choices too. We set no cookies.

When you connect an AI client

If you connect an MCP client such as Claude, we receive the tool calls it makes, which contain the same VINs and vehicle details as a web lookup. The client’s name and its sign-in tokens are held in server memory only. We do not receive your conversation.

Who receives data

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. To build a report, our server sends the VIN, or the make, model and year, to the public sources below. They receive the request from our server, not from you, so they do not see your IP address or your account.

  • NHTSA (U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration): vehicle specifications, recalls, complaints and safety ratings.
  • EPA (fueleconomy.gov): fuel economy and emissions.
  • Vehicle manufacturers’ recall lookups: for supported makes, the VIN is sent to the manufacturer’s own recall service to check that vehicle’s recall status.

Vehicle photos load from mcp.vin itself. They are NHTSA’s own photos of the vehicles it crash-tested, which we downloaded from NHTSA once and serve from our server, so showing them does not contact NHTSA or any image service. Your browser contacts NHTSA only if you open one of the linked NHTSA test reports or original photos.

  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces used on the site.

The service runs on a rented server in the United States. We would disclose data if the law required it.

Your choices

  • Use it without an account. Lookups, the API and the public MCP tools need no sign-up.
  • Remove saved vehicles one at a time from the Saved vehicles panel.
  • Delete your account from the account menu on the website: choose Delete account and confirm with your password. Your account, saved vehicles and settings are removed at once, and your account number is removed from the request logs.
  • Clear recent reports from the Saved vehicles panel, or by clearing site data in your browser. This also clears transmission choices kept in your browser.
  • Ask us. Email privacy@mcp.vin to get a copy of your data, to correct it, or to have it deleted if you cannot sign in. We answer within 30 days.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted scrypt hashes. Sign-in attempts and API use are rate limited. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affected your account we would tell you by email.

Children

mcp.vin is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or share, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. The page history is public in the project repository.
