01 / EPA VEHICLE CATALOG

Vehicle specifications and efficiency

No VIN needed

Select an EPA test configuration to review specifications, emissions, dimensions and owner-reported fuel economy. Configuration records do not establish a vehicle’s exact factory equipment.

02 / VEHICLE COMPARISON

VIN report comparison

Compare two to four distinct VINs. Separate entries with spaces, commas or new lines. Unavailable fields remain explicit; recall campaign counts should not be used to rank vehicle safety.

03 / SAFETY RESEARCH

Manufacturer communications and investigations

Search NHTSA's indexed service bulletins, campaigns, warranty communications and defect investigations. A bulletin is not a recall or proof of a defect in your vehicle.

04 / OWNERSHIP SCENARIO

Financing and ownership cost analysis

Enter purchase, financing and operating assumptions to calculate a payment schedule and ownership-cost breakdown. Default values are illustrative. Results are not lender quotes, tax determinations or repair forecasts.

Development priorities

  • Manufacturer-specific build-sheet and recall adapters, starting with verified public access.
  • Owner-supplied window stickers, with extracted facts linked to the original document.
  • Dealer opt-in inventory feeds with timestamps and permission to reuse photos.
  • Recall and bulletin changes between dated snapshots, with user-controlled alerts.
  • Region-specific tax rules and automated refresh for the dated rate benchmarks.
